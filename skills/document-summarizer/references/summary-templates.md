# Summary Templates

## Research Paper Summary

```markdown
# Paper Summary: [Title]

**Authors:** [Names] | **Published:** [Date] | **Venue:** [Journal/Conference]

## Abstract (from paper)

[Copy or paraphrase the abstract]

## Research Question

What question does this paper try to answer?

## Methodology

- **Approach:** [Experimental/Theoretical/Survey/etc.]
- **Data:** [What data was used, sample size]
- **Methods:** [Key techniques applied]

## Key Findings

1. [Finding 1 with supporting data]
2. [Finding 2 with supporting data]
3. [Finding 3 with supporting data]

## Contributions

- [What new knowledge this paper adds]
- [Practical applications if any]

## Limitations

- [Acknowledged limitations]
- [Scope restrictions]

## Future Work

[What the authors suggest for next steps]

## Relevance

[Why this paper matters for the reader's context]
```

## Legal/Contract Summary

```markdown
**Document Type:** [NDA/SLA/Employment/etc.]

## Purpose

[What this agreement is for]

## Key Terms

### Obligations

**[Party A] must:**

- [Obligation 1]
- [Obligation 2]

**[Party B] must:**

- [Obligation 1]
- [Obligation 2]

### Financial Terms

- **[Payment/Price]:** [Amount, frequency]
- **[Penalties]:** [If applicable]

### Term & Termination

- **Duration:** [Length]
- **Renewal:** [Auto/manual, conditions]
- **Termination:** [Notice period, conditions]

## Important Clauses

| Clause             | Summary                           | Section |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------- | ------- |
| Confidentiality    | [What's protected]                | §X      |
| Liability          | [Limits and caps]                 | §X      |
| Dispute Resolution | [Arbitration/Court, jurisdiction] | §X      |

## Red Flags / Notable Items

- ⚠️ [Unusual or concerning provision]
- ⚠️ [Another item requiring attention]

## Action Required

- [ ] [Signature/approval needed]
- [ ] [Information to provide]
```
